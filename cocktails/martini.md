---
name: Martini
---

- 45 ml dry gin
- 15 ml dry vermouth

Shaken or stirred - depending on how strong you want it - and strain into a martini glass.

There are hundreds of ways to make a martini, and everybody will say every recipe other than theirs is wrong.
I like mine clean with a decent splash of vermouth, but be sure to experiment and try out different proportions.
Most importantly though, be sure to use a good quality gin!
