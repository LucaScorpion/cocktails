---
name: Negroni
---

- 30 ml gin
- 30 ml Campari
- 30 ml sweet vermouth

Combine into a rocks glass with big ice cubes.

One of the all-time classics.
Bittersweet in the best possible way.

For a bit of a sweeter note, try it with a bourbon vanilla infused gin.
I've found that vanilla works surprisingly well to complement the sweetness and offset the bitterness a bit more.
